What is Bank Repo Cars?

Bank Repo Cars is a vehicle dealership in Bloemfontein. We sell vehicles at different prices and in different conditions. Some run and drive, some need repairs and some are non-runners. Every listing says which, so you always know what you are looking at.

How Bank Repo Cars works

  1. Browse. Look through the vehicles on the website. Each one has photos, a stock number, the price and a description of its condition and problems.
  2. Enquire. Press Inquire About This Vehicle and fill in your details. Your enquiry is saved and WhatsApp opens with your message ready to send to our sales team.
  3. Talk to us. Our sales team contacts you about the vehicle and answers your questions.
  4. Pay your way. Our vehicles are sold either for cash or on rent to own.

Cash or rent to own

Cash. You pay the full asking price shown on the vehicle.

Rent to own. Rent to own is for people who are blacklisted or who cannot get bank finance. You pay a deposit of 30% of the vehicle's price, then pay monthly over 48 or 54 months. Rent to own is only available on selected vehicles. Each of those vehicles shows its deposit and an estimated monthly installment.

Tracker insurance is included in the monthly installment. The monthly amounts shown on the website are estimates, shown as a range, and our sales team will confirm your exact installment.

Documents for a rent to own application

  • If you are employed: 3 months bank statements, 3 months payslips, driver's license, and ID or passport.
  • If you are self-employed or own a business: 6 months bank statements, driver's license, and ID or passport.
